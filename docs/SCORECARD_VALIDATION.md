## 2. The limitation that governs every number below

The data is generated by this repository. Every metric in this document
is a property of that generator, not of a lending population, and the
protected attribute used in the fair-lending section is a synthetic
overlay this repo constructs. **No result here is evidence the model
works on real applicants.** This section is second rather than last on
purpose.

Beyond that: the model is fit on FUNDED loans only, while the scoring
population is all applicants. Funded default rate is 28.28%; the true
all-applicant rate is 41.02%, visible only because the generator
produced it. Reject inference propagates an assumption about that gap; it
does not close it.

Three treatments a reviewer should check, and what this card does:

- **Missing is a LEVEL, not a hole.** `employment_type` is missing for
  9.0% of the build sample, and that missingness is not at random --
  it concentrates in the self-employed, who default more. Imputing the
  mode imports the W2 default rate onto the riskiest slice of the book.
- **Special codes are not quantities.** `credit_age_reported` carries the
  bureau's -9 no-hit code. Binned as a number it sorts below every real
  credit age and inherits the youngest-file risk, which is an artefact of
  the encoding. It gets its own bin.
- **Rare levels are merged, not scored.** A level with a handful of
  accounts produces a WoE that is noise, and a card that assigns it
  points is assigning points to noise.

| dti | 0.0070 | stable |
| inquiries_6m | 0.0008 | stable |
| delinq_2y | 0.0004 | stable |

**And this is where a PSI-only monitoring pack fails.** Over the same
span the actual default rate moved **+15.77%**, of which the model
predicted **+5.03%** -- leaving **+10.74% unexplained**. PSI found the
mix shift it is designed to find, and the mix shift is the smaller half.
Applicants defaulting more AT THE SAME characteristics is invisible to
any distribution statistic on those characteristics, by construction.

Monitoring plan implied by that: PSI is a supporting signal, and the
primary one is the vintage actual-versus-expected gap, which needs
outcomes and is therefore months late. There is no early label-free
signal for outcome shift, and pretending PSI is one is the error this
section exists to prevent.

## 6. Use limitations

- Rank-ordering only. The PDs are calibrated on the funded population and
  must not be used to price the rejected one.
- No override policy, no manual-underwrite path, no low-side/high-side
  override tracking.
- The approval cutoff (80% approval rate) is a policy input, not a
  model output, and belongs to credit policy rather than to modelling.
